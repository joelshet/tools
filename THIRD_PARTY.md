# Third-party software

`.vale/styles/` vendors three Vale style packages. proselint and write-good keep their license texts in their own directories (`.vale/styles/proselint/README.md`, `.vale/styles/write-good/README.md`).

## Google (`.vale/styles/Google/`)

Vale-compatible implementation of the Google developer documentation style guide.

Source: https://github.com/errata-ai/Google (MIT)

## proselint (`.vale/styles/proselint/`)

Vale-compatible implementation of the proselint linter.

Source: https://github.com/errata-ai/proselint (BSD-3-Clause), based on https://github.com/amperser/proselint

## write-good (`.vale/styles/write-good/`)

Vale-compatible implementation of the write-good linter.

Source: https://github.com/errata-ai/write-good (MIT), based on https://github.com/btford/write-good
